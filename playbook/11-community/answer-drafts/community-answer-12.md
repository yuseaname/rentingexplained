## Answer body (260 words)

Move-in day is the most important day of your lease. Here's the exact 60-minute sequence to protect yourself.

**Hour 1, Minute 0–10: Photos, top to bottom.**
- Wall, ceiling, floor in every room
- Every scratch, dent, stain (with a ruler in shot for scale)
- Every appliance — open/close them, run them
- Every outlet, light switch, smoke detector
- Every window — open, close, lock
- Timestamp on. Email the photos to yourself IMMEDIATELY.

**Hour 1, Minute 10–20: The move-in checklist.**
- Most landlords have a checklist. Walk it with them.
- Make sure pre-existing damage is marked.
- Take a video of the landlord acknowledging each item.
- Get the signed checklist. Email yourself a copy.

**Hour 1, Minute 20–30: Test everything.**
- All faucets (hot AND cold)
- All toilets (flush, run for 30 sec)
- All outlets (use a $10 outlet tester from Home Depot)
- All appliances (stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, fridge, washer, dryer)
- Heat + AC (run for 5 min each)
- Hot water (run for 5 min)

**Hour 1, Minute 30–40: Document utility readings.**
- Electric meter
- Gas meter (if applicable)
- Water meter
- Send photos to yourself.

**Hour 1, Minute 40–50: Lock and key.**
- Test every door, window, sliding door
- Change the locks (with permission) — $30 at any hardware store
- Re-key if you can't change — same cost

**Hour 1, Minute 50–60: Move-in day paperwork.**
- Confirm renter's insurance is active
- Confirm utilities are in your name
- Confirm mail forwarding is set
- Email yourself a "move-in complete" summary with photos

**If anything is broken, document it IN WRITING to the landlord THE SAME DAY.** Email + photo. This is your proof.
