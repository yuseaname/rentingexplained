## Answer body (260 words)

Even if your landlord doesn't require it, renters insurance is one of the highest-leverage $15–$25/month you'll ever spend. Here's why.

**What it covers:**
1. **Your personal property** — if your apartment burns down or is burglarized, your landlord's insurance covers the building, not your stuff. Without renters insurance, you replace your $10,000 of belongings out of pocket.
2. **Liability** — if a guest is injured in your apartment (slips on a wet floor, gets bitten by your dog), you could be sued for $50,000+. Renters insurance includes $100K–$300K of liability coverage.
3. **Loss of use** — if your apartment becomes uninhabitable (fire, flood), renters insurance pays for temporary housing (hotel, Airbnb) while repairs are made.
4. **Medical payments** — if a guest is injured, the policy pays their medical bills directly without a lawsuit.

**What it doesn't cover:**
- Flood (separate policy needed)
- Earthquake (separate policy)
- High-value items (jewelry, art, collectibles) — usually capped at $1,500; you may need a rider.

**Cost breakdown (2026):**
- Average US: $15–$25/month
- Bundled with auto insurance: $10–$18/month
- Deposit: 0 (most policies)

**The math:**
- $20/month × 12 = $240/year
- $10,000 of property coverage (10x return on annual cost)
- $100,000+ of liability coverage (500x return)

**Common objections:**
- "I don't own much." — Most people undervalue their stuff. Add it up: laptop, phone, furniture, clothes, kitchen gear. Easiest way to hit $10K.
- "I'll just save the money myself." — Self-insurance works if you have $20K+ in cash earmarked for a worst-case scenario. Most people don't.
- "My landlord has insurance." — Landlord's insurance covers the building, not your stuff.

How to pick a policy (3 questions):
1. What's the replacement cost vs. actual cash value? (Replacement cost is better.)
2. What's the liability ceiling? (At least $100K.)
3. Are there any sub-limits for specific items?

Full breakdown of policy types, what to look for, and a sample policy comparison: https://rentingexplained.com/blog/renters-insurance-cost-2025/
