# Link Magnet Outreach Templates — 2025 Rental Cost Index

This file contains three personalized outreach email templates for promoting the [2025 Rental Cost Index by State](/blog/2025-rental-cost-index-by-state) to housing blogs, personal finance sites, and tenant rights organizations. Each template is designed for a different audience and should be customized with the recipient's name, publication, and a specific reference to their recent work.

Before sending:
- Always send from a real person's name (not a generic inbox).
- Personalize at least two sentences per email — a recent article, a shared contact, a topic you both cover.
- Keep the email under 200 words. Editors are busy.
- Include the data link clearly, not buried.
- Follow up once, no more than twice.

### Targets to Build a List From

For Housing Blogs and Real Estate Publications:
- Search "rental market trends 2025" + guest post pitch lists
- Curated renter-newsletter roundups
- Sites that link to .edu or government housing data
- Apartment list, Zillow research, RentCafe blog, Apartment Therapy

For Personal Finance / Money Blogs:
- NerdWallet, The Penny Hoarder, Money Under 30, Financial Best Life, Making Sense of Cents
- Substack newsletters in the personal finance niche
- Reddit r/personalfinance contributors with active blogs

For Tenant Rights Organizations:
- National Low Income Housing Coalition
- National Housing Law Project
- Local tenant unions and housing rights nonprofits
- Law school housing clinics with public-facing content
- Legal aid society blog networks
