Contact Us

How to reach Renting Explained for general questions, editorial feedback, corrections, or partnership inquiries.

· Jan 1, 2025 · 2 min read

We’d love to hear from you. Whether you have a question, a content suggestion, or feedback on a guide, this page tells you how to reach us.

Get in touch

Email is the best way to reach us. Pick the address that matches your message:

  • General inquiries — hello@rentingexplained.com
  • Editorial & content suggestions — editorial@rentingexplained.com
  • Report an error — corrections@rentingexplained.com (see our Corrections page for what to include)
  • Privacy questions — privacy@rentingexplained.com
  • Partnership & business — partnerships@rentingexplained.com

We read every message. We aim to respond to most inquiries within a few business days, though we may not be able to reply to every single one.

Before you email about a legal question

Renting Explained provides educational content about renting. We are not able to give legal advice, represent tenants in disputes, or provide personalized consulting. If you have a legal dispute with a landlord, the best next step is usually:

  1. A local tenant-rights organization or renters’ hotline in your city or state;
  2. A legal aid society (many offer free help to income-qualified renters); or
  3. A qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.

You can often find these through your state or city housing department’s website.
