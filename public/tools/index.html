How these tools help

  • Budget before you browse. Knowing your real number keeps you from falling in love with a place you cannot afford.
  • Compare total move-in cost. First month + deposit + fees + moving can equal 3–4x monthly rent.
  • Spot lease problems early. A few clauses show up again and again in bad leases.
  • Protect your deposit. Documentation at move-out is one of the best ways to get your full deposit back.
