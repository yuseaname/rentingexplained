## The five steps

<div class="timeline">
  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="timeline-dot"></div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>1. Figure out what you can afford</h3>
      <p>The 30% rule is a starting point, but the real cost of renting includes deposits, utilities, insurance, and moving expenses. Use our <a href="/tools/rent-budget-calculator/">Rent Budget Calculator</a> to see the full picture.</p>
      <p><a href="/blog/how-much-rent-can-i-afford/">Read: How Much Rent Can I Afford? →</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="timeline-dot"></div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>2. Gather your documents</h3>
      <p>Landlords usually want ID, proof of income, bank statements, and references. If you are self-employed or have no rental history, you will need a few extras.</p>
      <p><a href="/blog/proof-of-income-for-apartments/">Read: Proof of Income for Apartments →</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="timeline-dot"></div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>3. Search and tour smart</h3>
      <p>Know where to look, what questions to ask, and which red flags mean you should walk away. Rental scams are common because renters are often in a hurry.</p>
      <p><a href="/blog/apartment-tour-checklist-questions-red-flags/">Read: Apartment Tour Checklist →</a> <br>
      <a href="/blog/rental-scams-how-to-avoid/">Read: How to Avoid Rental Scams →</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="timeline-dot"></div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>4. Read the lease like a pro</h3>
      <p>A lease is a contract. Know what each clause means, what you can negotiate, and which terms should make you pause before signing.</p>
      <p><a href="/blog/understanding-a-lease-agreement/">Read: Understanding a Lease Agreement →</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="timeline-item">
    <div class="timeline-dot"></div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>5. Document and move in</h3>
      <p>Protect your security deposit by photographing the unit at move-in, keeping receipts, and knowing how to report problems in writing.</p>
      <p><a href="/blog/first-apartment-checklist-guide-2025/">Read: Apartment Move-In Checklist →</a></p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
