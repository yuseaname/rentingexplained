# North Carolina renting laws

This page summarizes the key landlord-tenant rules for **North Carolina**. Local laws may add additional protections, so check your city or county housing department as well.

## Security deposit

2 weeks for week-to-week, 1.5 months for month-to-month, 2 months for longer.

## Landlord entry

No specific statute.

## Notice to vacate

Typically 30 days.

## Rent control / rent increases

No statewide rent control.

## Where to get help

- Search for a local tenant-rights organization or legal aid society in North Carolina.
- Contact your city or county housing department for local ordinances.
- For disputes, consider a qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.
