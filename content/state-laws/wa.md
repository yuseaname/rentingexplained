# Washington renting laws

This page summarizes the key landlord-tenant rules for **Washington**. Local laws may add additional protections, so check your city or county housing department as well.

## Security deposit

No cap, but a fixed 30-day return deadline applies since July 2023; deposit must be held in a trust account with receipt.

## Landlord entry

48 hours.

## Notice to vacate

Typically 30 days.

## Rent control / rent increases

No statewide rent control.

## Where to get help

- Search for a local tenant-rights organization or legal aid society in Washington.
- Contact your city or county housing department for local ordinances.
- For disputes, consider a qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.
