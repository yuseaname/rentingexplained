# Kansas renting laws

This page summarizes the key landlord-tenant rules for **Kansas**. Local laws may add additional protections, so check your city or county housing department as well.

## Security deposit

1 month rent for unfurnished, 1.5 for furnished.

## Landlord entry

Generally reasonable notice (often 24–48 hours).

## Notice to vacate

Typically 30 days.

## Rent control / rent increases

No statewide rent control.

## Where to get help

- Search for a local tenant-rights organization or legal aid society in Kansas.
- Contact your city or county housing department for local ordinances.
- For disputes, consider a qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.
