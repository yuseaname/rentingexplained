# Texas renting laws

This page summarizes the key landlord-tenant rules for **Texas**. Local laws may add additional protections, so check your city or county housing department as well. For the full walkthrough, see our [Texas tenant rights guide](/blog/texas-tenant-rights/).

## Security deposit

No statewide cap; market practice 1–2 months. Refund due within 30 days of surrender AND written forwarding address, with itemized deductions; bad-faith withholding = 3x + $100 penalty.

## Landlord entry

No statutory notice rule — set by the lease.

## Notice to vacate

3 days for nonpayment/lease violation (unless lease differs); 30 days for month-to-month termination.

## Rent control / rent increases

Rent control banned statewide (Loc. Gov't Code §214.902); no cap or notice requirement for increases; retaliation prohibited (§92.331).

## Where to get help

- TexasLawHelp.org — free legal information and aid referrals.
- Search for a local tenant-rights organization or legal aid society in Texas.
- For disputes, consider a qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.
