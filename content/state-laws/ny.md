# New York renting laws

This page summarizes the key landlord-tenant rules for **New York**. Local laws may add additional protections, so check your city or county housing department as well. For the full walkthrough, see our [New York tenant rights guide](/blog/new-york-tenant-rights/).

## Security deposit

One month's rent maximum (GOL §7-108). Refund due within 14 days of move-out with an itemized statement — missing the deadline forfeits all deductions.

## Landlord entry

Reasonable notice at a reasonable time; no fixed statutory hours (24 hours is standard practice). Emergencies exempt.

## Notice to vacate

30 days typical (varies by tenancy length); 14-day written rent demand required before nonpayment eviction.

## Rent control / rent increases

Rent stabilization in NYC and some localities; NYC Good Cause Eviction caps market-rate increases at inflation + 5% (max 10%) in covered units.

## Where to get help

- NYC tenants: call 311 or visit NYC HPD for repairs, violations, and Good Cause guidance.
- Search for a local tenant-rights organization or legal aid society in New York.
- For disputes, consider a qualified tenant-landlord attorney in your jurisdiction.
